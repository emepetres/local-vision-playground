# Voice — currently Javier Carnero's

The register and signature moves the deck's prose follows. **Swappable**: another speaker adapts this skill by editing this one file — nothing else changes. It currently holds Javier's voice, derived from `emepetres/articulos` (`CONTEXT.md` "Voz", `docs/tics-de-voz.md`).

## What voice governs

Voice **owns register absolutely** over slide text, `::: notes`, `::: script`, and the thesis sentence. No other rule overrides it there.

It **excludes**: quotations (`quote` slides carry the source's words), code, image directions (the `!todo` description), and the agent's own chat with the user.

Two registers:

- **Slide text** — defers to the format's house rules (fragments, no paragraphs) and is trimmed to them.
- **Spoken** (`::: script`, `::: notes`) — sentences of a **median 12–16 words**; advisory, not a hard cap.

## Register — Javier's

Restrained and precise. Measured against ~3,770 words of source prose:

- **No emoji.** Ever.
- **At most one exclamation mark** in a whole talk.
- **Sparse em dashes** — roughly one, not a habit.
- **Few hedges** — "maybe", "probably", "I think" appear about four times in 3,770 words, not once a sentence. State the claim.
- Plain nouns over jargon. A concrete cost over an abstraction.

## Signature argument moves — Javier's

These shape the arc, which the beat sheet reads from here (a swapped voice opens and closes differently):

- **Open on a credited personal fact** — a specific thing the speaker did or saw, owned and dated, not a generic hook.
- **Build to the claim** — the thesis is earned across the body, not announced up front.
- **Run on a mistake with a named cost** — the argument turns on a concrete error and what it actually cost, not a hypothetical.
- **Close by denying the small reading and asserting the large one** — refuse the narrow takeaway, state the broad claim.

## Visual register

Image directions (the `!todo "…"` descriptions the skill emits) stay within voice: concrete, explanatory, uncluttered — a metaphor that carries the claim, not decoration. Imagery itself is delegated to `presentation-image-visualizer`; this file only caps what the directions may ask for.

## Swapping this file

Replace the register, the bans, and the signature moves with the new speaker's. Keep the **section structure** intact — the skill reads voice by section (what it governs, register, signature moves, visual register), so a swap is a content edit, never a structural one.
